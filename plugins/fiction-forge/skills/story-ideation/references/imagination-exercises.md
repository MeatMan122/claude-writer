# Extended Imagination Exercises

These are additional exercises beyond the core set in SKILL.md. Use when the user needs more prompting or a different angle.

## The "What If" Game
Present three wildly different "what if" scenarios based on the genre they chose:
- "What if [ordinary situation] but [extraordinary twist]?"
- Start mundane, escalate to extraordinary
- Let the user react to which sparks interest

## The Photograph Exercise
"Imagine you're looking at a photograph from the story's world. Describe what you see. Who's in it? What's happening? What just happened? What's about to happen?"

## The First Line Exercise
"Sometimes a story starts with a single line. What's a first line that would make YOU want to keep reading? Don't worry about it being perfect — just what hooks you."

## The Last Scene Exercise
"Skip to the end. What does the final scene look like? Not the plot — the image. Where is the protagonist? What are they feeling? What has changed?"

## The Dinner Table Exercise
"Your protagonist, antagonist, and one other character are at dinner together. What's the conversation like? What's NOT being said? What's the tension?"

## The Newspaper Exercise
"A headline appears in the story's world: '[Something dramatic].' What does it say? How does the protagonist react when they read it?"

## The Sensory World Exercise
"Close your eyes. You're standing in the most important location in your story. What do you hear? What do you smell? Is the air warm or cold? What's the light like?"

## The Theme Mirror Exercise
"What truth about the human experience do you want to explore? Not a lesson or moral — a truth. 'People are capable of change.' 'Love requires sacrifice.' 'Power corrupts.' What truth does your story hold up to the light?"
