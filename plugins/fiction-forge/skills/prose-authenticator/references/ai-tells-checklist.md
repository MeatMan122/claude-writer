# AI Writing Tells: Complete Checklist

## Vocabulary Tells
- Overuse of: delve, tapestry, intricate, nuanced, embark, beacon, labyrinth, testament, multifaceted, paradigm, landscape (metaphorical), pivotal, realm (non-fantasy), foster, endeavor, resonate, underscore, myriad, plethora, dichotomy, juxtaposition, visceral, palpable
- Cliché intensifiers: "utterly," "absolutely," "profoundly," "deeply"
- Pseudo-intellectual: "one might argue," "it bears mentioning," "it is imperative"

## Structure Tells
- Every paragraph follows: topic sentence → elaboration → conclusion
- Lists of three in almost every description
- Transitions between every paragraph (Moreover, Furthermore, However)
- Each sentence builds on the previous in a neat, logical chain
- Everything feels "balanced" — no rough edges, no surprises
- Summarizing what just happened before moving on

## Emotional Tells
- "A wave of [emotion] washed over..."
- "His heart hammered in his chest"
- "She couldn't help but feel..."
- Stacking emotional metaphors: "a storm of grief, a tsunami of regret"
- Telling emotion through physical clichés: "tears streamed," "fists clenched," "jaw tightened" (all in the same paragraph)

## Descriptive Tells
- Purple prose: "The ethereal moonlight cascaded across the gossamer curtains"
- Describing everything in precise, beautiful detail (humans are selective)
- Sensory descriptions that feel catalogued rather than natural
- Over-qualifying: "a somewhat large, rather imposing, slightly weathered building"

## Dialogue Tells
- Every character speaks in complete, grammatical sentences
- Characters explain their feelings openly: "I feel betrayed because you..."
- Dialogue that reads like an essay: logical, structured, comprehensive
- Everyone is articulate and eloquent
- No verbal tics, no speech errors, no interruptions

## Narrative Voice Tells
- The narrator is always measured, balanced, and fair
- No strong opinions or personality in the narration
- Hedging: "seemed," "appeared," "perhaps," "somewhat"
- Explaining the subtext instead of letting it exist
- Never committing to a tone (serious one paragraph, light the next)
