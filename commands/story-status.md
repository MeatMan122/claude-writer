---
description: Check your story project's current progress
---

Find the story project directory in the user's workspace by looking for directories containing a `PROJECT.md` file. Read `PROJECT.md`.

Present a concise summary of:
- **Current phase** and step (ideation, outlining, writing, etc.)
- **Progress** (chapters written out of total, outline status, etc.)
- **Key decisions** made so far (structure, genre, POV, etc.)
- **Open questions** or decisions needed from the user
- **What happens next** — what the next step in the process is

If multiple projects are found, show status for all of them. If none found, let the user know and offer to start one.
